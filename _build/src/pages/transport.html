Transport questions

Do you own trucks?

No. Skylink doesn't own trucks or sell goods. We connect cargo owners with transporters, and transporters with loads.

Which routes do you cover?

Any route across Africa, within one country or across borders. Nairobi ⇄ Mombasa, Mombasa ⇄ Kampala and Lagos ⇄ Abuja are just examples. Tell us your route and we'll search for you.

Do you guarantee a return load?

No. We look for a return load on every request and connect you when one is available. Sharing your route early gives us the best chance.

What truck types can join?

Any type: flatbed, box body, tipper, container/trailer, refrigerated, tanker and more.

What does Skylink charge?

Our commission is 7% of the transport value of the load.

How are transporters paid?

Payment terms are set per load and shown before you accept it. On most of our loads it is 70% at loading and 30% after delivery; some loads pay 80% at loading and 20% at destination.

Do you offer long-term contracts?

Yes. Alongside single trips we post contract work, from 3 months to 5 years, for transporters who want consistent cargo. Some contracts start with a few trucks and scale up.
