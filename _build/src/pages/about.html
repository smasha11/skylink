Two services. One network.

Transport & load matching. Truck owners tell us where they are and where they're going. We find a load for the trip, and a return load when available, so trucks don't run empty. Cargo owners tell us what they need to move, and we connect them with a suitable truck.

Our founder's story

Skylink Global Network was founded by Sara Mohamed with a simple conviction: good business should be accessible, honest, and built on trust.

In Kenya, opportunity is often lost in the gaps between people. Truck owners deliver a load and then spend days calling and messaging to find the next one, sometimes driving back empty. Buyers struggle to find reliable suppliers, and quality suppliers abroad struggle to reach the right buyers. Sara saw that the real problem wasn't a lack of trucks, cargo or products. It was a lack of trusted connections.

So she built Skylink to close those gaps: a network that connects transporters with cargo, and buyers with suppliers across Kenya, Nigeria, India, China and beyond.

Today, Skylink stands for verified connections, transparent communication, and a warm, inclusive community. We take time to understand every request, we connect people directly, and we run our business on honest, halal principles, because trust is at the heart of every deal we support.
