FAQ

Trade questions

Do you sell the products yourselves?

No. Skylink doesn't sell goods. We connect buyers with suitable suppliers, and you deal with each other directly.

Which countries do buyers come from?

Our buyers are currently in Kenya and Nigeria, and we're growing beyond.

Which countries do suppliers come from?

China, India, Japan and other markets. We have verified connections in India for tiles, sanitary ware, rice and spices.

What do I need to send as a buyer?

Product + Quantity + Destination, plus any specifications (size, grade, packaging). That's enough for us to start checking.
