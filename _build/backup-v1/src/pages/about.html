About Skylink

We simply connect the right people.

Skylink Global Network is a B2B connection business based in Nairobi, Kenya. We don't own trucks and we don't sell goods. We find the right match and connect you directly.

Business | Travel | Logistics | Opportunities

Who we are

Two services. One network.

Transport & load matching. Truck owners tell us where they are and where they're going. We find a load for the trip, and a return load when available, so trucks don't run empty. Cargo owners tell us what they need to move, and we connect them with a suitable truck.

B2B trade & supplier sourcing. Buyers in Kenya and Nigeria tell us the product, quantity and destination. We check the right supplier from India, China, Japan and other markets, and help connect them.

Our mission

Moving businesses forward.

Every empty truck and every unanswered enquiry is lost business. We save our clients time and open more opportunities by connecting them with the right people, directly.
