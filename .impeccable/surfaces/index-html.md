# Surface brief: Skylink marketing site (Home + inner pages)

Scope: whole site redesign, Persuade mode. Audience: truck owners first (WhatsApp from an Android phone), then cargo owners, buyers across Africa, suppliers. Action: send a pre-filled WhatsApp request. Proof: none confirmed; no stats, quotes or logos; photos are stock scenes and say so. Constraints: pinned black/yellow palette, Anton + Poppins, exact taglines, transparent nav over the hero photo (user request), static HTML on Hostinger. Direction chosen unattended after the decision page went unanswered and the user said "finish the design".

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every page is printed like a kanga cloth: a border, a central field, and Skylink's message where the proverb goes. Refuses the freight-broker hero + stats + logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: Black cloth printed in one yellow. The user removed the printed border strips (pindo) on 2026-10-22 because they pulled attention from content; do not reintroduce frames. Message bands (ujumbe) are black fields between double yellow rules carrying the taglines in Anton capitals; sections alternate dark (black) and light (white/grey) per the user's brief: "Alternate dark (black) and light (white/grey) sections the way our posts do." Load 1 prints solid, Load 2 prints dashed.

STORY: A driver sees his road framed like a cloth he knows, reads the message, taps Have a load? / Have a truck?, and sends one WhatsApp message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed truck photo, no frame; transparent nav over it. Dispatch bar (Have a load? | Have a truck?) left at mid-height, primary. The message band "Real connections. Real movement." spans the bottom above the border. Stock caption seamed to the photo corner.
